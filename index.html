IIA Liberating Education

The original works of history’s greatest minds.

Our students study the original works of history’s most influential authors, across the disciplines — mathematics, science, language, literature, philosophy, and theology — via small, vigorous classroom discussions.

Read the books. Then talk about them.

No textbooks, no lectures, no majors — one integrated curriculum, read whole and argued over around the seminar table.
